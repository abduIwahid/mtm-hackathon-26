Terms of use

This is a demonstration tool built for MTM Hackathon '26 by Team AXIONS. By using it you accept these terms.

What the tool does

The planner reads a pack of event messages that you provide, extracts the facts stated in them, and computes a plan - venue, schedule, food, judging and budget - with checks, a fact ledger, and risk recommendations. The plan is only as good as the messages you give it.

Verify before you rely

  • The reading model can misread a message. The built-in checks catch many errors, but they are not a guarantee. Verify every figure against your source messages before spending money or making commitments.
  • Figures, prices and availability in any plan are computed from your input at the moment you run it. They are not quotes, bookings or confirmations from any venue, caterer or provider.
  • Recommendations are generated from the plan's own figures to help you review it. They are suggestions, not professional advice.

  • You are responsible for the content you paste or upload, and for having the right to send it to the model provider you choose.
  • You are responsible for your API key and any charges or limits on your own account with the provider.
  • Do not use the tool to plan anything unlawful or unsafe.
